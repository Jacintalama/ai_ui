About

Design, code, and a little AI.

Freelancing for Swiss & US clients · open to new work

I'm a tech-savvy, creative software engineer based in Maasim, Sarangani. I hold a Bachelor's in Information Technology from Holy Trinity College of General Santos City, and I specialize in full-stack web development and AI-driven solutions — building scalable Next.js apps and automation systems with Claude Code, Puppeteer, and LLM workflows.

Right now I deliver end-to-end builds for two international clients: a Swiss e-commerce platform and a US-based Open WebUI deployment. I scope the requirements, plan the architecture, and ship production-ready AI features in iterative releases. Alongside that, I work as a graphic artist at Starbright Printing Press — calendars, business cards, company IDs, vector identity work, and BIR ATP documentation.

Before freelance I was the web developer at General Santos City Water District, building an EPANET.js-style tool to process .INP files for hydraulic and water-quality modeling. I'm certified NC III in Visual Graphic Design and NC II in Electronic Products Assembly & Servicing — the design eye and the IT-support fundamentals both show up in the work.
