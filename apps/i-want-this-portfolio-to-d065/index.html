/ about

A short bio

I've been building software since 2020, starting on frontend and backend at Hiretalk, then moving into Python services and ML-assisted automation at Mid Atlantic Distribution. From 2022 to 2025 I worked as an AI Software Engineer at the American Board of Anesthesiology, engineering token-cost optimization layers, evaluation harnesses, and structured-output reliability for multi-step agentic workflows. Since mid-2025 I've been an AI Engineer (Title Contract), architecting production-grade LLM platforms and using Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) to expose internal systems to Claude agents.

Most of my work sits at the load-bearing layer: retrieval grounding, hybrid search, agent reliability, eval harnesses, and the routing/fallback policies that decide whether an AI feature is a demo or a product. I care about systems that fail loudly, prompts that read like specifications, and confidence checks that catch silent regressions before users do.
