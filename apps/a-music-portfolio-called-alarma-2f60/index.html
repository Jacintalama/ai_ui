About

Alarma is the alias of a Lisbon-based producer who started out playing piano in church basements and ended up building drum machines from broken radios. The work moves between three rooms — a tiny home studio for sketches, a shared warehouse space for mixing, and whichever venue happens to be open after midnight.

Releases have appeared on independent labels across Portugal, Germany, and Japan, with sync placements on two feature documentaries and an indie game. Currently finishing a debut LP and scoring a short film about lighthouse keepers — out late 2026.
