About Test Water

Test was founded in 2014 by a small team of hydrologists and outdoor enthusiasts who believed that the water you drink every day should be as clean, balanced, and refreshing as the water they grew up drinking straight from mountain streams. We set out to build a brand that put source quality first — no industrial processing, no shortcuts, just water as nature delivers it.

Today, Test is sold in over 1,200 grocery stores, cafés, and gyms across the country. Every bottle is filled within 60 metres of the spring it comes from, sealed without contact with the open air, and shipped in 100% recycled PET that uses 40% less plastic than industry standard bottles.

We believe great drinking water shouldn't be a luxury. It should be the easy, obvious, healthy choice — every single day.

From spring to bottle

Our water travels less than 100 metres from where it emerges from the earth to where it's sealed in your bottle. Here's how it gets there.

01

Rainfall

Rain and snowmelt collect high in the alpine watershed, untouched by farms or industry.

02

Natural filtration

Water seeps through 800m of limestone and granite over 30+ years, picking up natural minerals.

03

Spring emergence

It surfaces at our protected spring at a constant 8°C — crisp, clean, and rich in electrolytes.

04

Sealed bottling

Drawn directly into bottles within 60 metres of the source, never exposed to open air.

Bottled responsibly

We're committed to delivering pure water without leaving a trail of waste behind. Here's what that looks like in practice.

♻️

100% recycled PET

Every bottle is made from rPET — recycled bottles given a second life — and is fully recyclable again after use.

🌱

Carbon-neutral shipping

We offset 100% of transportation emissions through verified reforestation projects in the watershed we draw from.

💧

Watershed protection

We protect 1,800 hectares of land around our spring through a partnership with the regional conservation trust.
