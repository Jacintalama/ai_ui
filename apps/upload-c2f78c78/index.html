the house style

// principles clarity over cleverness
latency is a feeling
defaults are decisions
density rewards trust
motion explains state
keyboards still matter

AI products fail at the seam between the model and the user. The model is improbably good; the interface is improbably forgettable. We design that seam.

Our work tends toward the legible. Not minimalist for its own sake — information-dense when density helps, restrained when it doesn't. We believe operators and power users are an underserved aesthetic tradition, and we draw from it heavily.

We ship in code. Designs that survive only in Figma are designs that haven't been pressure-tested. Every engagement ends with something running.
