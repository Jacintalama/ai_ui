About the studio

Lumen Studio was founded in 2019 by Maya Okafor and Daniel Reiss after a decade of working together at larger product agencies. We started with a simple thesis: the best work happens when the same small group of people who frame the problem also write the code that ships.

We deliberately stay small — five full-time designers and engineers, no project managers, no account leads. You talk to the people doing the work, every day. Most weeks that means a Tuesday demo, a shared Figma file, and a Linear board you have full access to.

We work in six-week cycles inspired by Shape Up, take on roughly one new engagement a month, and turn down any work we don't think we can do exceptionally well. That's the only filter we apply.
