# Landing page

**Scope:** Full landing page for Crumb and Co bakery  
**Mode:** Persuade

## Audience and task

Basel locals and visitors looking for quality sourdough bread and pastries. They should understand what makes Crumb and Co special and be motivated to visit the physical bakery.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** This landing page celebrates the honest craft of bread-making through clean Swiss design sensibility, refusing the predictable warm-cream-and-serif bakery template. The visitor sees the bakery's confidence in its work.

**OWN-WORLD:** Flour white as the primary ground with wheat gold and deep crust brown as accent colors. Clean sans-serif typography that echoes Basel's design heritage. Natural linen and wood textures used sparingly as material truth, not decoration. Simple, bold, structured layouts that let the bread be the hero.

**STORY:** Visitor lands on a confident opening that shows what Crumb and Co makes. They learn about the sourdough process and craft, see what's available, and find the bakery's location and hours. Primary action is visiting the physical location.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Bold bakery name at top with clean navigation. Hero section shows the core offer (artisan sourdough and pastries from Basel) with imagery of actual bread. Clear call-to-action to visit or see location. The composition is structured and clean, not cozy or cluttered.

**FORM:** Code-led build. Swiss-inspired grid structure, confident typography hierarchy, natural material palette. Direct and honest rather than nostalgic.

**FINISH:** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
