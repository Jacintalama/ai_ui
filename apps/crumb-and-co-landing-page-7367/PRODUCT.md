# Crumb and Co

A landing page for a small artisan bakery in Basel, Switzerland.

## What it is

Crumb and Co is a local bakery specializing in sourdough bread and pastries. The landing page introduces the bakery to people in Basel looking for quality baked goods and encourages them to visit the physical location or browse the products.

## Who it's for

- People in Basel looking for sourdough bread and pastries
- Local residents seeking artisan baked goods (inferred)
- Visitors to Basel interested in local food experiences (inferred)
