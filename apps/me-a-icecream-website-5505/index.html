Our Story

Scoops & Sprinkles started in 2017 as a single push-cart at the Saturday farmers' market. Maya, our founder, was a pastry cook who got obsessed with the question: why does so much ice cream taste like sweetened air?

The answer, mostly, was stabilizers and overrun. So we ditched both. Our base is just whole milk, heavy cream, egg yolks, and cane sugar — churned slowly enough that you can taste the dairy.

Today we run one tiny shop on Mulberry Street and ship pints anywhere in the lower 48. Same recipes. Same farmers. Same stubborn opinions about sprinkles (the long ones, never the round ones).
