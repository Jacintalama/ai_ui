AI Agents

Build an assistant that works the way you want: give it a name, tell it what to do and how to behave, and choose whether it can reach the apps you have connected. Your agents are yours, and they show up wherever you already chat.

Being built
1

Write the instructions

Name the agent and describe what it should do and how it should answer. Plain sentences, not settings. You see exactly what you typed, and you can change it whenever you like.

2

Give it your tools

One switch lets an agent use the apps you connected under Connections, acting as your account and nobody else's. Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Documents and the rest can be turned on one at a time.

3

Use it anywhere

An agent you build appears in your model picker, so it works in chat, in Discord and Telegram, and on a schedule. Nothing extra to set up.

Private by default. An agent you create is visible only to you. There will also be a few ready-made agents shared with everyone, which you can use as they are or copy and make your own.
