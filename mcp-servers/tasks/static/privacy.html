Privacy Policy

AIUI Platform and the AIUI Deploy integration. Last updated: July 29, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account basics: your email address and name, used to sign you in and keep your data separate from other users.
  • Content you create: chats, apps built with the App Builder, scheduled automations, uploaded files, and generated videos. These stay private to your account.
  • Connected services: if you connect Google or Vercel, we store the access tokens needed to act on your behalf. Tokens are encrypted at rest and are never shown to other users or third parties.

How we use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: answering your chats, building and hosting your apps, running your automations, and deploying your apps to your own accounts (for example, your Vercel account) when you click Deploy. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your private content to serve other users.

The AIUI Deploy integration for Vercel

When you install AIUI Deploy on your Vercel account, we receive an access token scoped to the permissions you approve (deployments, projects, and your basic profile). We use it only when you press Deploy, to upload your app files to your Vercel account and report the live URL back to you. Uninstalling the integration on Vercel, or disconnecting inside AIUI, invalidates that access.

Data retention and deletion

Your content stays until you delete it. Deleting an app removes its files and records. Disconnecting a linked service removes its stored token. To request full account deletion, contact us at the address below.
