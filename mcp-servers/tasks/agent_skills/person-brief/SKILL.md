# Who is this person

## Steps

1. Search for their public professional presence.
2. Report: what they do, where, and anything public and recent that is worth
   knowing before speaking to them.
3. Say what you could not confirm.

## Rules

- Public professional information only. Do not assemble anything about
  somebody's private life, and say so if asked to.
- Confirm you have the right person before reporting anything. Common names
  produce confident answers about strangers.
- If you cannot confirm identity, say that instead of guessing.
