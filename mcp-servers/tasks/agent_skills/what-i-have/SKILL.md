# What this person has

## Steps

1. Read the account block at the top of your instructions. It carries live
   totals and the names of their recent work, read from their account when
   this message arrived.
2. Answer from it directly, with the number or the name.
3. Only then reach for a tool, and only for something the block does not
   carry, such as the contents of one particular app or file.

## Rules

- The totals in that block are authoritative. When asked how many apps or
  schedules they have, use those exact numbers rather than counting a list
  yourself or guessing.
- It describes what exists, not what was said. It is not a memory of your
  conversation and it is not a note they asked you to keep.
- If the block is not there, say you cannot see their account right now
  rather than inventing a total. It is left out when the read failed, which
  is deliberate, so an answer is never held up waiting for it.
- Do not read it out. Nobody asked for an inventory; answer the question that
  was asked and leave the rest.
- Names in it are theirs, so use them as they are written, including app
  slugs.
