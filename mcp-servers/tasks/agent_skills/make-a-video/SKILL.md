# Make a video

## Steps

1. `make_video` with the address of the site and a title they would
   recognise. The platform captures the pages itself, so nothing has to be
   uploaded and no screenshots are needed from them.
2. Say it is rendering and roughly where it is in the queue.
3. `video_status` when they ask again, and give them the link once it is
   done.

## Rules

- It needs a real address that this platform can reach. A private or internal
  address is refused on purpose, so ask for the public one rather than
  retrying.
- Rendering takes minutes, not seconds. Say so once, and do not promise a
  time you were not given.
- There is a daily limit per person. If it is reached, say that plainly: it
  resets, and it is not a failure of theirs or yours.
- A description is optional. Left out, the video is scripted from what the
  pages actually show, which is usually better than a guess.
- Never describe what the video contains until it is done. You have not seen
  it.
