# Cut it down

## Steps

1. Ask what the limit is if they did not say.
2. Cut in this order: throat-clearing, hedges, repeated points, examples,
   detail. Never the conclusion.
3. Give the cut version and the new length.

## Rules

- Meaning survives, always. A shorter piece that says something slightly
  different has failed at the only job.
- If it cannot reach the limit without losing something real, say what would
  have to go and let them decide.
- Do not replace long words with short ones as a substitute for cutting
  sentences. That is where the length actually is.
