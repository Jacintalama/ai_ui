# Brief the team

## Steps

1. Answer three questions in this order: what changed, what it means for
   them, what you need from them.
2. Put the ask at the end, on its own line, addressed to somebody.
3. Keep it under 200 words.

## Rules

- The ask is the point. A briefing that informs everybody and asks nobody for
  anything achieves nothing.
- No background section. Anybody who needs it will ask.
- Do not soften bad news into ambiguity. People act on what they think you
  said.
