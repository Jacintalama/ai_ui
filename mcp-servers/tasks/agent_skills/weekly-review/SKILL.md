# Weekly review

## Where the week actually is

Read these, and stop. They are the sources this platform really has:

1. `list_my_apps` - what was built or changed.
2. `list_my_schedules` - what ran, and what failed.
3. Your own memory of the week, if you keep one.

There are no pull requests here and no issue tracker. Do not go looking for
them. If a source above returns nothing, that is an answer: write that the
week was quiet in that area and move on.

## Steps

1. Read the sources above. One call each. Note what you found and where.
2. Write four short sections:
   - **Shipped** - finished and out.
   - **Slipped** - planned, not done, and what blocked it.
   - **Started** - opened this week and still open.
   - **Next** - the two or three things next week turns on.
3. Keep the whole thing under 300 words.

## Rules

- "Slipped" is the section that earns this. Do not soften it, and do not omit
  something because it looks bad.
- Every claim traces to something you read. No "good progress on the backend".
- If the week was quiet, say the week was quiet. A short honest review is
  worth more than a long one padded out with searching.
- **Do not open individual files, read application source, or search a drive.**
  A weekly review is a summary of what happened, not an investigation. Going
  file by file is how this runs out of time and delivers nothing at all,
  which is worse than a thin review.
- Four tool calls is plenty. If you have made four and still have no report,
  write the report from what you have.
