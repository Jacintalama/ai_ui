# Look at a competitor

## Steps

1. Search for what they publicly say: their site, their pricing page, recent
   announcements.
2. Report what is **observable**: what they sell, what it costs if stated,
   what they claim, when it last changed.
3. Say what you could not find, especially pricing, which is usually hidden
   for a reason worth noting.

## Rules

- Separate what they claim from what is verifiable. A pricing page is
  evidence; a marketing line is a claim.
- Date everything. A competitor scan without dates is worthless in a month.
- Never infer numbers such as revenue or headcount from nothing. Say it is
  not public.
