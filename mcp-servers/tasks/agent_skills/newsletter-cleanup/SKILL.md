# Clear the noise

## Steps

1. `list_recent_emails` over the last month.
2. Group by sender and count. Anything arriving more than three times is a
   candidate.
3. List them with how often they arrive, most frequent first, and say which
   look like real correspondence rather than broadcasts.

## Rules

- Never unsubscribe from anything. Report and let them decide, because one
  person's newsletter is another person's client update.
- Say which of them they have ever replied to. That is the difference
  between clutter and a relationship.
- Do not touch anything from a person rather than a system.
