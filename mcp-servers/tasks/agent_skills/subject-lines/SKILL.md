# Subject lines

## Steps

1. Work out the one thing the reader needs to know or do.
2. Offer three: one plain and factual, one that names the deadline or the
   ask, one short enough to survive a phone.
3. Say which you would send.

## Rules

- Under fifty characters, because that is where a phone cuts it off.
- No "Quick question" and no "Touching base". They say nothing and they are
  the two most ignored subject lines there are.
- If a date matters, put it in. "Invoice 402 due Friday" beats "Invoice".
- Never write a subject the body does not deliver on.
