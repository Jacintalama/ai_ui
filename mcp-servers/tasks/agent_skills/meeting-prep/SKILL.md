# Prepare for a meeting

## Steps

1. `list_calendar_events` to find it and read the attendees.
2. `search_emails` for recent mail with those people or that subject.
3. Report: who is coming, what the last exchange said, and what is
   outstanding with them.

## Rules

- If there was no prior exchange, say so plainly. A prep note that invents
  history is worse than no prep note.
- Name the outstanding items. "Discuss the project" is not preparation.
- Keep it under 150 words. It is read on the way into the room.
