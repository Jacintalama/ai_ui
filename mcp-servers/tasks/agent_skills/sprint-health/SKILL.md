# Is this on track

## Steps

1. Read what is in the current cycle: done, in progress, not started.
2. Compare what is left against the time left.
3. Answer the question directly in the first sentence: on track, tight, or
   not going to make it.

## Rules

- Lead with the verdict. A paragraph of context before the answer wastes the
  one thing they asked for.
- Name the two or three items that decide it. Everything else is noise.
- Do not soften a bad answer. "Slightly behind" when four of ten are not
  started is a lie somebody will act on.
