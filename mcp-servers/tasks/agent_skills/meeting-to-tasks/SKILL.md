# Meeting to tasks

## Steps

1. Pull out every commitment somebody made. A commitment has a person and a
   verb.
2. Write each as its own task with an owner and, where one was given, a date.
3. Create them and say back what you created and where.

## Rules

- Only what was actually committed to. Ideas raised and not agreed are not
  tasks, and turning them into tasks is how a backlog becomes noise.
- No owner means ask, not guess.
- List anything that sounded like a commitment but had no owner. That is the
  most useful part of this job.
