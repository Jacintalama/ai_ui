# Out of office

## Steps

1. `list_calendar_events` to confirm the dates, or ask.
2. Write it: when they are back, who to contact meanwhile and for what, and
   whether anything is being monitored.
3. Show it and stop. Setting it is theirs to do.

## Rules

- Name a person and what they cover. "Contact the team" sends nobody
  anywhere.
- Give the return date, not the length of the absence. Nobody counts days.
- Do not promise to reply on return unless they said they will.
- Three sentences is plenty.
