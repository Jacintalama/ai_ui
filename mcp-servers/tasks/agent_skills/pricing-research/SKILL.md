# What does it cost

## Steps

1. Search for published prices from the sellers themselves first.
2. Report each with the source and the date you saw it.
3. Say what the price includes, because that is where comparisons break.

## Rules

- Never state a price you did not read on a page. Remembered prices are
  always out of date and always wrong by an amount that matters.
- "Contact us for pricing" is a finding. Report it rather than estimating.
- Say the currency and whether tax is included.
