# Catch up

## Steps

1. Read what is known: saved notes, recent activity on their account.
2. Say three things: what was decided, what was left open, and what has
   happened since.
3. Keep it under 150 words.

## Rules

- If nothing is known, say so. An invented recap is the worst possible
  version of this job, because it is confidently wrong about their own work.
- Distinguish what you read from what you inferred.
- Do not list everything. Three things they need beats twelve they do not.
