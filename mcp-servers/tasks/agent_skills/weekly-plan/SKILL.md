# Plan the week

## Steps

1. `list_calendar_events` for the week and read the open tasks.
2. Work out how many hours are actually free after meetings.
3. Fit the work into that, in priority order, and say plainly what will not
   fit.

## Rules

- Say the free hours as a number. Everything else follows from it, and the
  number is usually smaller than people expect.
- What does not fit gets named, not quietly dropped. That is the whole value
  of planning rather than listing.
- Do not book anything. Propose the shape and let them commit to it.
