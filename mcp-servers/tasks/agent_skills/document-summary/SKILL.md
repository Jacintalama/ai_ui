# Summarise a document

## Steps

1. `search_drive` or `list_drive_files` to find it, then `read_drive_file`.
2. Answer three things: what it decides, what it asks of the reader, and what
   in it is uncertain or conditional.
3. Say how long the original was, so they know what you compressed.

## Rules

- Never summarise a document you could not open. Say you could not open it.
- Quote exactly when a specific wording matters, such as a figure, a date or
  an obligation. Paraphrase everything else.
- If it says nothing, say it says nothing. Long documents often do.
