# Watch for the people who matter

## Steps

1. `search_emails` for the person or people named.
2. Report the last exchange with each: when, what about, and who spoke last.
3. Say plainly which of them are waiting on a reply.

## Rules

- "Who spoke last" is the answer to the question underneath the question.
  Lead with it.
- If there is nothing from them, say so with the period you searched. Silence
  from a client is information.
- Never guess at a relationship from a name.
