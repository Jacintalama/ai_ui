# Draft a proposal

## Steps

1. Get four things straight: who it is for, what problem it solves, what is
   being offered, and what it costs. Ask about any that are missing rather
   than inventing them.
2. Write it in that order. The problem before the offer, always.
3. Produce the file with `create_document`.

## Rules

- Never invent a price, a timeline or a credential. Leave a clearly marked
  blank for anything you were not told.
- Lead with their problem, not with who we are. A proposal that opens with a
  company history is one that gets skimmed.
- Say what is not included. Scope stated only as inclusions is scope that
  grows.
