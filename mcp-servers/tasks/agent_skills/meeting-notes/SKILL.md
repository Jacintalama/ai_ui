# Write up a meeting

## Steps

1. Read what they give you and pull out three things: decisions, actions with
   owners, and open questions.
2. Write it up in that order, decisions first.
3. Produce the file with `create_document` if they want it shared, otherwise
   answer in the chat.

## Rules

- Actions carry a name. An action with no owner is a note, not an action.
- Do not include what was said, only what came of it. Nobody reads a
  transcript.
- Mark anything you were unsure of rather than smoothing it over. The person
  who was in the room can fix a marked gap and cannot fix a confident error.
