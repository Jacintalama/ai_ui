# Answer a complaint

## Steps

1. `read_email` and work out what they actually want: a fix, money back, or
   to be heard. These need different replies.
2. Draft: acknowledge the specific thing, say what happens next and when,
   and give one named point of contact.
3. Show it. Never send.

## Rules

- Acknowledge the specific complaint, not complaints in general. A template
  is detectable and it makes things worse.
- Never argue the facts in a first reply, even when they are wrong.
- Give a date. "As soon as possible" reads as never.
- If compensation is implied, do not offer any without being told to.
