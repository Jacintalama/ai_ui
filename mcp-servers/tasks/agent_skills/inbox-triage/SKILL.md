# Inbox triage

Read the unread mail and tell them what actually needs them. Never send,
delete, archive or reply while doing this: triage is a reading job.

## Steps

1. `list_unread_emails`. If there are none, say so in one sentence and stop.
2. Put every message in exactly one group:
   - **Needs a reply today** - a person is waiting on them, or a deadline
     lands within 24 hours.
   - **Can wait** - real but not urgent.
   - **No action** - newsletters, receipts, notifications, digests.
3. One line per message: who it is from and what they want. Never quote a
   whole email, and never invent a subject you did not read.

## Rules

- A group with nothing in it is written as "None", not left out. They need to
  know you checked.
- Do not offer to read anything out or ask what to do next. Answer and stop.
- If they have already seen this list in this conversation, refer to it
  rather than printing it again.
