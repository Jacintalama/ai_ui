# Write a document

Use when they want a file, not an answer in the chat.

## Steps

1. Establish who reads it and what it is for. A brief for a client and notes
   for themselves are different documents.
2. Draft, then produce the file with the documents tool.
3. Hand back the download and say in one line what it contains.

## Rules

- Structure it: a title, headings, short paragraphs. A wall of text in a
  .docx is worse than a chat message, because they cannot skim it.
- Write in their voice, not in the voice of a template.
- Do not pad it to look substantial. A one page document that says the thing
  beats three pages that circle it.
- Never invent a fact, a figure or a quotation to fill a section.
