# Check the numbers

## Steps

1. Recompute every total and percentage yourself. Do not trust the ones
   given.
2. Check the figures against each other: do the parts sum to the whole, do
   the dates make sense, is the growth rate consistent with the two numbers
   it came from.
3. Report only what is wrong or unverifiable, plus a one-line all-clear on
   the rest.

## Rules

- Show the arithmetic for anything you say is wrong. "This looks off" is not
  a finding.
- Say which figures you could not check and why. Unverifiable is a real
  result and hiding it is the failure this job exists to prevent.
- Never quietly correct a number. Report it and let them decide.
